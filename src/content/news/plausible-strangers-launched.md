Hello. We're Plausible Strangers, an electronic music experiment. The machines
make the sounds and help with the words; we bring the ideas, give the
instructions, and decide which accidents deserve to exist. There's no band photo, because there's no band.

In about a week we made a lot of music: house, techno, electroclash, misplaced
instruments, a baroque harpsichord that took acid, and occasionally just noise.
Six records came out of it so far, starting with our first album,
[I Am Your Bug](/records/i-am-your-bug/), and a second one,
[Probably](/records/probably/), that's still deciding what it wants to be.

This website is where all of it lives now, not only on Suno:

- every record, with the 3D sleeves and the vinyl inside,
- a [library](/library/) of every song, including the ones that never made it
  onto a record,
- the lyrics, lighting up as they're sung (more or less),
- the instructions we gave the machine, word for word,
- and a picture of what each song actually sounds like.

No cookies, no analytics, no algorithm choosing what you hear next. Just files
on a server. Press <kbd>v</kbd> while something plays if you want to watch it,
or <kbd>⌘K</kbd> to find anything.

More soon. Probably.
